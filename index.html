About SolveUp

Infrastructure Management for Smart Cities

SolveUp is an AI-powered infrastructure management system that helps citizens report public infrastructure issues while enabling authorities to monitor, prioritize and resolve them efficiently.

The platform improves transparency, reduces delays, prevents fake closures, and enables data-driven governance.
